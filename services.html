Our 5 Core Services

Screen printing is one of the best options for bulk custom apparel where design consistency and long-lasting quality are important. It is widely used for college fest T-shirts, corporate staff uniforms, event merchandise, and team wear. For simple to medium-color artwork, this method gives excellent value and is often the most affordable for larger quantities. At Lobo Prints, we focus on proper setup, curing, and quality checks so the print remains durable after repeated washes. If you need cheap and best-value bulk printing in Bangalore with quick planning support, screen printing is usually the first recommendation.

DTF (Direct to Film) is ideal for high-detail and multi-color designs where precision and color richness matter. It works well for startup merch, custom logo combinations, detailed artwork, and short to medium quantity runs. DTF is flexible across fabric types and is a practical choice when you need both quality and fast turnaround. For customers searching nearby for quick and premium-looking custom print output in Bangalore, DTF provides strong visual results while keeping production practical.

Embroidery gives a premium and professional finish for polos, jackets, caps, and uniforms. It is preferred by offices, institutions, schools, and hospitality teams that need long-term branding with a high-quality look and feel. Thread-based logo finishing offers strong durability and elegant texture. We digitize logos carefully so output is neat even in smaller stitch areas. If you are comparing embroidary or embroidery services in Bangalore, this method is ideal when brand presentation and durability are priorities.

Sublimation is the best fit for polyester apparel and all-over sports designs. It is commonly used for jerseys, training kits, and performance wear where breathability and color vibrancy are important. Since the dye bonds into the fabric, the print remains lightweight and resistant to cracking or peeling. This method is excellent for sports teams and events with sponsor-heavy or full-coverage graphics. For fast and consistent jersey production in Bangalore and Karnataka, sublimation is a proven choice.

In-house garmenting helps maintain better control over fit, finish, shade consistency, and production timelines. It reduces dependency on external vendors and improves reliability for repeat bulk orders. This is a major advantage for colleges, businesses, and teams that need dependable quality over multiple batches. The in-house workflow also helps us support quicker timeline commitments when deadlines are tight. If you are looking for nearby custom printing with stable quality and practical pricing, in-house garmenting creates strong value.
