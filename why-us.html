The Lobo Prints Difference

6 Reasons We're the Right Choice

Whether it's ten T-shirts for a startup or a thousand jerseys for a school tournament, here's why we never let you down.

Reason 01

Uncompromising Print Quality

Every garment goes through a multi-step quality check. We use premium inks and heat-press calibration to ensure your logo or artwork looks pixel-perfect — wash after wash. Fading, cracking, or bleeding? Not here.

From cricket clubs and school sports days to corporate uniforms and startup merch — we've served 500+ clients across Bangalore and Karnataka. Our repeat client rate speaks for itself.

Reason 03

In-House Production, Zero Middlemen

We own our printing infrastructure. No outsourcing = tighter quality control, faster delivery, and better prices. You deal directly with the people making your products.

Reason 04

Deadlines We Actually Keep

We know your event has a fixed date. Our team plans production timelines backward from your deadline, not forward from when it's convenient. For bulk orders, delivery is typically completed in around 10 days.

You get a clear, itemised quote upfront. No hidden setup fees, no late surcharges, no "we forgot to mention" extras. What we quote is what you pay — and it's competitive.

T-shirts, hoodies, jerseys, trackpants, caps, mugs, ID cards, badges, jackets, sweatshirts — all under one roof. You save time, coordination hassle, and money by bundling orders with us.
